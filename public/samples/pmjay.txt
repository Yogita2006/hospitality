                    SCHEME TERMS AND CONDITIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════




1. NATURE OF ENTITLEMENT


1.1  PM-JAY is a package-based assurance scheme. There is no
     individual sum insured in the indemnity sense. The scheme
     provides cover of Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year on a
     floater basis, utilised against defined Health Benefit
     Packages (HBP 2.0).


1.2  Payment to the hospital is made at pre-agreed package rates
     notified by the National Health Authority. The beneficiary
     pays nothing at the point of care.


1.3  A package rate is inclusive of the entire episode of care.
     It covers consultation, diagnostics, bed charges, nursing,
     surgeon and anaesthetist charges, medicines, consumables,
     implants where specified, food for the patient, and follow-up
     care for the defined post-operative period.


1.4  Hospitals are prohibited from charging the beneficiary any
     amount over and above the package rate for a covered
     procedure. Any such demand should be reported to the State
     Health Agency grievance channel.




2. ROOM ENTITLEMENT


2.1  Beneficiaries are entitled to General Ward accommodation.
     Room charges are included within the package rate and are not
     billed separately. There is no per-day room rent limit
     expressed in rupees.


2.2  Upgrade to a higher room category is not covered under the
     scheme. Where a beneficiary opts for a higher category, the
     entire admission moves outside the scheme and becomes payable
     by the beneficiary.


2.3  For the avoidance of doubt, the scheme does not operate a
     partial or proportionate settlement in such cases. The
     admission is treated as a private admission in its entirety.


2.4  Where a hospital is unable to provide a General Ward bed and
     accommodates the beneficiary in a higher category for
     operational reasons, the package rate remains unchanged and
     the beneficiary bears no additional cost.




3. INTENSIVE CARE


3.1  ICU care, where clinically indicated, is included within the
     applicable package rate. No separate ICU per-day limit
     applies.


3.2  Certain high-end critical care packages carry their own
     defined rates under HBP 2.0 and may be claimed where the
     clinical criteria specified in the package definition are met.




4. CO-PAYMENT AND DEDUCTIBLE


4.1  PM-JAY is a fully cashless scheme. No co-payment is payable by
     the beneficiary.


4.2  No deductible applies.


4.3  Transport allowance, where notified by the State Health
     Agency, may be paid to the beneficiary on discharge.




5. PROPORTIONATE DEDUCTION


5.1  Not applicable. Package rates are fixed and are not scaled
     against room category.




6. WAITING PERIODS


6.1  There is no waiting period. All conditions, including
     pre-existing diseases, are covered from the first day of
     entitlement.


6.2  There is no age limit and no restriction on family size under
     the scheme.




7. EXCLUSIONS


The following are not covered:


7.1  Treatment at hospitals not empanelled under PM-JAY.


7.2  Out-patient department (OPD) consultations and procedures.


7.3  Cosmetic procedures.


7.4  Individual diagnostics not forming part of a package.


7.5  Drug rehabilitation programmes.


7.6  Fertility related procedures.




8. HOSPITAL NETWORK


8.1  Treatment is available ONLY at hospitals empanelled under
     PM-JAY. Treatment at a non-empanelled hospital is not
     reimbursable under any circumstance.


8.2  The list of empanelled hospitals is published by the National
     Health Authority and the State Health Agency and is subject to
     periodic revision. Beneficiaries are advised to verify
     empanelment status before admission.


8.3  Both public and private empanelled hospitals may be accessed.
     Certain packages are reserved for public hospitals under the
     State's implementation guidelines.




9. AVAILING TREATMENT


9.1  The beneficiary should approach the Ayushman Mitra help desk
     located at the empanelled hospital.


9.2  Identity is verified against the Ayushman card and a
     government photo identity document. Biometric or OTP based
     verification may be used.


9.3  The hospital raises a pre-authorisation request against the
     applicable package. On approval, treatment proceeds cashless.


9.4  On discharge, the beneficiary signs the claim documentation.
     No payment is collected from the beneficiary.




10. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL


10.1 Beneficiaries may raise grievances through the national
     helpline 14555, the Centralised Grievance Redressal Management
     System, or the District Grievance Redressal Committee.


10.2 Complaints regarding denial of treatment, demand for payment,
     or refusal of admission by an empanelled hospital should be
     raised immediately through the Ayushman Mitra desk or the
     State Health Agency.


10.3 Action against empanelled hospitals for violation of scheme
     conditions is taken by the State Health Agency in accordance
     with the empanelment agreement.
