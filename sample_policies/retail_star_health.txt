PREAMBLE


WHEREAS the Insured Person named in the Schedule has made to the
Company a proposal which is hereby agreed to be the basis of this
contract, and has paid the premium specified in the Schedule, the
Company agrees, subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions and
definitions contained herein, to indemnify the Insured Person in
respect of expenses incurred as hereinafter provided.




SECTION 1 - DEFINITIONS OF STANDARD TERMS


1.1  "Accident" means a sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event
     caused by external, visible and violent means.


1.2  "Any One Illness" means a continuous period of illness including
     relapse within 45 days from the date of last consultation with
     the Hospital where treatment was taken.


1.3  "Cashless Facility" means a facility extended by the Company to
     the Insured Person where the payment of the costs of treatment
     undergone by the Insured Person in accordance with the policy
     terms is made directly to the Network Provider by the Company
     to the extent pre-authorisation is approved.


1.4  "Co-payment" means a cost sharing requirement under a health
     insurance policy that provides that the policyholder will bear
     a specified percentage of the admissible claim amount. A
     co-payment does not reduce the Sum Insured.


1.5  "Deductible" means a cost sharing requirement under a health
     insurance policy that provides that the Company will not be
     liable for a specified rupee amount in case of indemnity
     policies, which will apply before any benefits are payable.


1.6  "Hospital" means any institution established for in-patient
     care and day care treatment of illness and/or injuries and
     which has been registered as a Hospital with the local
     authorities under the Clinical Establishments (Registration and
     Regulation) Act 2010, or complies with the minimum criteria of
     at least 10 in-patient beds in towns having a population of
     less than 10,00,000 and 15 in-patient beds in all other places.


1.7  "Network Provider" means Hospitals enlisted by the Company to
     provide medical services to an Insured Person on payment by a
     cashless facility.


1.8  "Pre-Existing Disease" means any condition, ailment, injury or
     disease that is diagnosed by a physician within 48 months prior
     to the effective date of the policy, or for which medical
     advice or treatment was recommended by or received from a
     physician within 48 months prior to the effective date.


1.9  "Room Rent" means the amount charged by a Hospital towards room
     and boarding expenses and shall include the associated medical
     expenses.


1.10 "Sum Insured" means the pre-defined limit specified in the
     Schedule. Sum Insured on floater basis means the maximum limit
     under the policy for and amongst all Insured Persons named in
     the Schedule during the policy period.




SECTION 2 - SCOPE OF COVER


2.1  In-Patient Treatment
     The Company shall indemnify medical expenses incurred for
     hospitalisation of the Insured Person during the policy period
     for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours, provided that
     such expenses are Reasonable and Customary, and shall include:


     (a) Room, boarding and nursing expenses as provided by the
         Hospital, subject to the limit specified at Section 3.1;
     (b) Intensive Care Unit charges, subject to Section 3.2;
     (c) Surgeon, anaesthetist, medical practitioner, consultants
         and specialist fees;
     (d) Anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation theatre charges and
         surgical appliances;
     (e) Medicines, drugs and consumables;
     (f) Diagnostic procedures and diagnostic imaging modalities;
     (g) Cost of prosthetic and other devices or implants implanted
         internally during a surgical procedure.


2.2  Pre-Hospitalisation
     Medical expenses incurred during the 60 days immediately
     preceding the date of admission are payable, provided the
     in-patient claim is admissible.


2.3  Post-Hospitalisation
     Medical expenses incurred during the 90 days immediately
     following the date of discharge are payable, provided the
     in-patient claim is admissible.


2.4  Day Care Treatment
     Medical expenses for day care procedures where the treatment is
     taken in a Hospital or day care centre in less than 24 hours
     because of technological advancement are payable.


2.5  Road Ambulance
     Expenses incurred on road ambulance are payable subject to the
     sub-limit specified in Section 5.


2.6  AYUSH Treatment
     Expenses incurred for in-patient treatment under Ayurveda,
     Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy systems of
     medicine in a recognised AYUSH Hospital are payable up to the
     Sum Insured.




SECTION 3 - LIMITS OF INDEMNITY


3.1  Room, Boarding and Nursing Expenses
     Room rent is limited to Rs. 5,000 per day or actuals, whichever
     is lower. The Insured Person is entitled to a Single Private
     A/C Room. Where the Hospital does not follow differential
     billing based on room category, this limit shall nonetheless
     apply to the room component of the bill.


3.2  Intensive Care Unit
     ICU charges are limited to 2% of the sum insured per day. Where
     an Insured Person is admitted to an Intensive Care Unit, the
     limit under Section 3.1 shall not apply for the duration of the
     ICU stay, and the limit under this Section shall apply instead.


3.3  PROPORTIONATE DEDUCTION


     Where the Insured Person is admitted to a room category whose
     rent exceeds the eligible room rent limit specified at Section
     3.1 above, the Company shall be liable to pay only a
     proportionate share of the total associated medical expenses,
     calculated in the ratio of the eligible room rent to the actual
     room rent incurred.


     Illustration: If the eligible room rent is Rs. 5,000 per day
     and the Insured Person occupies a room charged at Rs. 10,000
     per day, the Company shall be liable for 50% of the associated
     medical expenses.


     For the purpose of this Section, "associated medical expenses"
     shall include surgeon fees, operation theatre charges,
     anaesthetist charges, nursing charges and consultant visit
     charges.


     Proportionate deduction shall NOT apply to the cost of
     medicines, pharmacy items, consumables, implants and diagnostic
     investigations, which shall be payable at actuals subject to
     policy limits.


     Proportionate deduction shall not be applied in respect of
     Hospitals which do not follow differential billing, or where
     differential billing is not adopted for the specific line items
     of the bill.


3.4  Reasonable and Customary Charges
     The Company shall not be liable for any amount in excess of
     charges that are reasonable and customary for the geographical
     area in which treatment is rendered.




SECTION 4 - CO-PAYMENT AND DEDUCTIBLE


4.1  A co-payment of 10% shall apply to every admissible claim under
     this policy. The co-payment shall be computed on the amount
     admissible after applying all other terms, conditions and
     deductions of this policy, including but not limited to the
     proportionate deduction under Section 3.3 and the sub-limits
     under Section 5.


4.2  No deductible is applicable under this policy.


4.3  The co-payment shall be borne by the Insured Person and shall
     not be reimbursable under any circumstance.

SECTION 6 - WAITING PERIODS


6.1  Initial Waiting Period
     30 days from the date of commencement of the first policy,
     except in case of accidental injury. This waiting period shall
     not apply to subsequent renewals without a break.


6.2  Pre-Existing Diseases
     Pre-existing diseases shall be covered after 36 months of
     continuous coverage with the Company. Coverage under this
     Section is subject to the pre-existing disease having been
     declared at the time of proposal and accepted by the Company.


6.3  Specific Ailments
     A waiting period of 24 months applies to the following, when
     the condition is not pre-existing:


     (a) Hernia of all types
     (b) Cataract
     (c) Benign prostate hypertrophy
     (d) Joint replacement surgery (other than due to accident)
     (e) Hysterectomy for menorrhagia or fibromyoma
     (f) Piles, fistula and fissure in ano
     (g) Varicose veins and varicose ulcers


6.4  Maternity Expenses
     Maternity expenses are covered after 36 months of continuous
     coverage. Expenses for the first two living children only are
     covered.




SECTION 7 - PERMANENT EXCLUSIONS


The Company shall not be liable to make any payment in respect of:


7.1   Cosmetic and aesthetic treatment of any description, including
      but not limited to cosmetic surgery, unless necessitated by an
      accident, burn or cancer.


7.2   Dental treatment, unless arising out of an accident and
      requiring hospitalisation.


7.3   Treatment taken outside India.


7.4   Expenses arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy,
      hostilities, civil war, rebellion, or nuclear perils.


7.5   Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, or any
      addictive condition and consequences thereof.


7.6   Expenses for spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.


7.7   Any treatment received in convalescent homes, rest homes,
      health hydros, nature cure clinics or similar establishments.


7.8   Sterility, infertility and assisted conception procedures.


7.9   Change of gender treatments.


7.10  Vitamins and tonics unless forming part of treatment for an
      injury or disease certified by the attending medical
      practitioner.


7.11  Expenses related to any admission primarily for enforced bed
      rest, diagnostics or evaluation purposes only.


7.12  Participation in hazardous or adventure sports.


7.13  Breach of law with criminal intent.




SECTION 8 - NETWORK AND CLAIMS PROCEDURE


8.1  Cashless Facility

8.2  Pre-Authorisation
     For planned hospitalisation, pre-authorisation must be sought

     hospitalisation, intimation must be given within 24 hours of
     admission.


8.3  Non-Network Hospitalisation
     Treatment at a non-network hospital shall be reimbursed at 100%
     subject to policy terms, on submission of original documents
     within 15 days of discharge.


8.4  Documents Required
     Duly completed claim form, original discharge summary, original
     hospital bill with break-up, payment receipts, investigation

SECTION 9 - GENERAL CONDITIONS


9.1  Free Look Period

9.2  Renewal

9.3  Portability
     The Insured Person may port the policy to another insurer at
     the time of renewal, subject to IRDAI (Health Insurance)
     Regulations. Application for portability must be made at least
     45 days before the renewal date.


9.4  Cumulative Bonus
     A cumulative bonus of 10% of the Sum Insured shall be added for
     each claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50%.


9.5  Nomination
     The Policyholder is required to appoint a nominee at the
     inception of the policy. Change of nomination may be made at
     any time during the policy period by written notice.


9.6  Cancellation
     The Company may cancel this policy on grounds of fraud,
     misrepresentation or non-disclosure of material facts, by
     giving 15 days notice. The Insured Person may cancel at any
     time, and refund shall be made on short period rates.


9.7  Fraud
     If any claim is found to be fraudulent, all benefits under the
     policy shall be forfeited.

10.3 The Insured Person may also approach the Insurance Ombudsman

     Ombudsman may be approached for disputes relating to partial or
     total repudiation of claims, delay in settlement, premium
     disputes, and non-issuance of documents.


10.4 Arbitration
     If any dispute arises as to the quantum payable under this
     policy, such dispute shall be referred to arbitration in
     accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. It
     is a condition precedent that an award shall first be obtained
     before any right of action against the Company shall accrue.
