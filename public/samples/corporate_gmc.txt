PART A - HOSPITALISATION BENEFITS


A.1  Room Category Entitlement


     No capping on room rent is applicable under this Group Policy.
     Members may avail any room category including Single Private
     A/C and Deluxe, subject to the overall Sum Insured.


     The Employer has opted for the enhanced room entitlement
     variant at the time of policy placement. Members are advised
     that suite and executive suite categories, where offered by a
     Hospital at rates materially above the prevailing market rate
     for a deluxe room, may be reviewed by the TPA for
     reasonableness.


A.2  Intensive Care Unit


     No separate capping on ICU charges is applicable. ICU expenses
     are payable at actuals subject to the Sum Insured.


A.3  Proportionate Deduction


     Proportionate deduction is WAIVED under this Group Policy.
     Since no room rent limit applies, associated medical expenses
     shall be payable in full subject to the Sum Insured.


     For the avoidance of doubt, no scaling of surgeon fees,
     operation theatre charges, nursing charges or consultant
     charges shall be applied by reference to the room category
     occupied by the member.


A.4  In-Patient Expenses Covered


     (a) Room, boarding and nursing charges
     (b) Intensive care unit charges
     (c) Surgeon, anaesthetist and consultant fees
     (d) Operation theatre charges and anaesthesia
     (e) Medicines, drugs and consumables
     (f) Diagnostic procedures and imaging
     (g) Implants and prosthetic devices used during surgery


A.5  Pre and Post Hospitalisation


     Expenses incurred 30 days prior to admission and 60 days
     following discharge are payable, provided the in-patient claim
     has been admitted.


A.6  Day Care Procedures


     All day care procedures listed by the TPA are covered without
     the 24-hour hospitalisation requirement.




PART B - CO-PAYMENT AND DEDUCTIBLE


B.1  No co-payment is applicable for treatment at Network Hospitals.


B.2  A co-payment of 20% shall apply ONLY where treatment is taken
     at a hospital outside the Network. Members are encouraged to
     use the Network to avoid this cost sharing.


B.3  No deductible is applicable.


B.4  Where a member is admitted to a non-network hospital in an
     emergency and subsequently transferred to a network hospital,
     the TPA may waive the non-network co-payment at its discretion
     on production of supporting documentation.

Pre-natal and post-natal expenses are payable within the applicable
maternity sub-limit and not in addition to it.


New born baby cover is available from day one of birth within the
family floater Sum Insured, subject to the baby being added to the
Certificate within 30 days of birth.




PART D - WAITING PERIODS


D.1  Initial waiting period is WAIVED for this Group Policy. Members
     are covered from the date of joining the scheme.


D.2  Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one under the Group
     Policy. No waiting period applies.


D.3  Maternity benefit is available from day one. There is no
     waiting period for maternity under this Group Policy.


D.4  The waiver of waiting periods applies only for the duration of
     the member's employment. On exit from the scheme, continuity
     benefits are subject to the portability provisions at Part G.




PART E - EXCLUSIONS


The following are not covered under this Certificate:


E.1   Cosmetic surgery not necessitated by accident or illness.


E.2   Infertility and assisted reproduction treatment.


E.3   Any treatment not requiring hospitalisation.


E.4   Out-patient consultations, except where covered under a
      separate OPD wallet benefit offered by the Employer.


E.5   Dental treatment other than that necessitated by accident.


E.6   Expenses for spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.


E.7   Treatment of alcoholism and drug dependency.


E.8   Experimental or unproven treatment.


E.9   Treatment taken outside India, unless the Employer has opted
      for the international cover extension, which is not opted
      under this Master Policy.




PART F - NETWORK AND CLAIMS


F.1  Cashless Treatment
     Cashless treatment is available across the Company's Network
     Hospitals through the appointed Third Party Administrator. The
     member must present the health card issued by the TPA along
     with a government photo identity document at the time of
     admission.


F.2  Pre-Authorisation
     Planned admissions require pre-authorisation at least 72 hours
     in advance. Emergency admissions must be intimated to the TPA
     within 24 hours.


F.3  Reimbursement
     Reimbursement at non-network hospitals is permitted at 80% of
     admissible expenses. Claim documents must be submitted to the
     TPA within 30 days of discharge.


F.4  Claim Documents
     Claim form, discharge summary, itemised hospital bill, payment
     receipts, investigation reports, treating doctor's prescription
     and the member's bank details for NEFT settlement.




PART G - GENERAL CONDITIONS


G.1  Continuity on Exit
     On cessation of employment, the member ceases to be covered
     under this Master Policy with effect from the last working day.
     The member may port to an individual retail policy with the
     Company, subject to underwriting and to IRDAI (Health
     Insurance) Regulations.


G.2  Mid-Term Additions
     Newly joined employees and newly married spouses may be added
     mid-term. New born children may be added within 30 days of
     birth.


G.3  Nomination
     The member is required to record a nominee on the employee
     benefits portal.


G.4  Non-Disclosure
     Benefits under this Certificate may be denied where material
     information has been withheld or misrepresented.


G.5  Free Look Period
     The free look provision does not apply to group policies
     placed by an Employer.




PART H - GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL


H.1  Members should first raise concerns with the Employer's HR
     benefits desk, which is the designated first point of contact
     for scheme-related queries.


H.2  Unresolved grievances may be escalated to the TPA grievance
     cell, and thereafter to the Company's Grievance Redressal
     Officer at the registered office.


H.3  If the grievance remains unresolved for 15 days, the member may
     approach the IRDAI Grievance Call Centre or the Integrated
     Grievance Management System.


H.4  The member may also approach the Insurance Ombudsman appointed
     under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017. The territorial
     jurisdiction of the Ombudsman is determined by the location of
     the Company's branch office or the residence of the member.


H.5  Arbitration
     Disputes as to quantum shall be referred to arbitration under
     the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
